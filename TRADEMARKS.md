# ScholarTap name and logo

The code is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`). The **name "ScholarTap", the ScholarTap logo and the tap-wave icon**
are not covered by that license. They belong to Ankush Sharma, who reserves them for the official project.

You may:

- say truthfully that your card, fork or service is "built on ScholarTap" or "compatible with ScholarTap";
- keep the "Developed by Ankush Sharma · University of Georgia" credit, which every card shows by default.

Without written permission you may not:

- run a public or commercial service called ScholarTap, or a confusingly similar name;
- use the logo or icon as the brand of a fork, product or company.

## University marks

"University of Georgia", "UGA", the Arch and Bulldog red (`#BA0C2F`) belong to the University of Georgia.
ScholarTap uses them only for UGA's own deployment, as UGA allows. They are kept in templates and copy that can be
switched off (`brand` in `card.json`), so a non-UGA or commercial version ships without them.
