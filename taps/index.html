Burn your NFC card

Hold a blank NFC card or sticker to the back of your phone. Your card link is written in a second, and any phone that taps it opens your ScholarTap.

This browser can't write NFC.

Writing works in Chrome on Android. On iPhone, copy your link and write it with the free NFC Tools app (Write → Add a record → URL). Any iPhone can read your card with no app.

Tip: NTAG213/215 cards and stickers cost about $1. Your link never changes, so a burned card keeps working when you edit your ScholarTap.
