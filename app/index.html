FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Ask GSA Connect at the next General Assembly.

Is ScholarTap really free?

Yes. Your card is hosted on GitHub Pages, which is free. NFC cards cost about $1 if you want a physical one. Wallet passes need the GSA's developer accounts, which members never pay for.

Do I need a GitHub account?

Yes, a free one. It's what keeps your card yours: your profile lives in your own repository, and your link never changes. Publishing walks you through it in under two minutes.

Can I change my card later?

Any time. Open your-link/app/, choose Edit my card, change what you like, including the template, and save. Your QR codes and NFC tags keep working.

Does NFC work with iPhones?

iPhones read ScholarTap tags instantly, with no app. To write a tag you need Chrome on Android, or the free NFC Tools app on iPhone. The writer page explains both.

What happens to my GitHub token?

It stays in your browser and is only sent to GitHub to create and update your card. Nothing is stored on any ScholarTap server, because there isn't one. You can revoke it any time in GitHub settings.

Is this an official UGA service?

ScholarTap is a student-led initiative of GSA Connect, part of the UGA Graduate Student Association. It is not an official University of Georgia website.
